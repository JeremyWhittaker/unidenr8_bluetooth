# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the evidence,
not the person holding it.

## What is expected

- **Grade your claims.** This project's whole method is separating what was
  observed from what was inherited or guessed. "I think" and "I measured" are
  both welcome; presenting the first as the second is not.
- **Accept correction gracefully**, and offer it the same way. Several of the
  best changes in this repository came from someone pointing out that a
  confident statement was not actually supported.
- **Respect the privacy rules.** Do not post other people's Bluetooth addresses,
  coordinates, or saved locations, and do not ask anyone else to.
- **Respect the safety rules.** Do not encourage anyone to build an unlicensed
  transmitter, speed to produce a capture, or operate any of this from the
  driver's seat of a moving vehicle.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, or unwelcome attention of any
kind. Publishing someone's private information. Sustained disruption of
discussion.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately to the maintainer if the
issue itself would be the problem. Reports will be handled promptly and
discreetly. The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, and may block
accounts, for behaviour that conflicts with this document.
